FAQ

Common Questions.

Everything you need to know about starting your English journey.

How does the AI Tutor work? expand_more
The AI Tutor is your personal 24/7 English teacher. You can choose different topics (like Business or Travel) and skill levels. It supports both Text and Audio voice notes, allowing you to seamlessly practice your reading, typing, listening, and speaking skills all in one place.
Is this platform suitable for beginners? expand_more
Absolutely! We support all levels ranging from absolute beginners (A1) to advanced proficiency (C2). The AI dynamically adjusts its vocabulary, grammar complexity, and speaking speed based on the level you select.
Can I practice my speaking and pronunciation? expand_more
Yes. By switching to the Audio Mode in the AI Tutor, you can use your microphone to speak naturally. The AI will understand your voice and reply back with a native-sounding voice note of its own.
Is the platform free to use? expand_more
Yes! Currently, all of our core lesson modules, AI text and audio chatting, dynamic quizzes, and personalized analytics are 100% free to use.
